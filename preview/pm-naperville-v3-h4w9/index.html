FAQ

Questions? We’ve Got Answers.

1How much does pest control cost?

General pest control starts from $150 for the initial service. We confirm your exact price on the phone before you book. Mice and rats, bed bugs and specialty services such as wildlife and termite (WDI) inspections are priced separately.

2What if I have children or pets?

Let us know when you book. Your technician plans the treatment around your family and pets, and explains any prep and when it’s fine to go back into treated areas.

3Which pests do you help with?

Common household pests including ants, cockroaches, spiders, fleas, ticks, flies, gnats, crickets, silverfish, earwigs, mites, stink bugs and carpet beetles, as well as mice and rats, bed bugs, and wasp, hornet and yellowjacket problems. This location also offers termite/WDI inspections, wildlife inspections, dead animal removal, commercial pest control and vehicle pest inspections.

4How soon can I arrange a visit?

Availability varies. Call the team to check the next open appointment.

5Do you serve my area?

We serve Naperville, Wheaton and many nearby communities, by ZIP code. Include your ZIP code in your request, or call, and the team will confirm your address and service.

6What if I don’t know which pest it is?

Leave “Not sure yet” selected on the form, or call and describe what you’ve noticed.

7What should I do before a visit?

Preparation depends on the service. The team will explain the instructions for your appointment.

8Do you treat termites?

This location provides termite/WDI inspections and reports; it does not provide termite treatment.
