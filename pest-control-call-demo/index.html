1The decisions

Three decisions on the sample call

Sample call. Names and details are made up.

  1. Capture what the caller reports

    Scratching in the kitchen wall and droppings under the sink, first noticed roughly a week ago. We write down what the caller describes; we don’t name the pest.

  2. Check the address and your booking rules

    14 Oak Lane is inside your service area, and a new-customer visit is a job your rules let us book.

  3. Offer an approved slot, or hand it off

    We offer tomorrow 10am–12pm from your approved slots. An address outside your area, or a request that needs your judgment, would go to your named contact instead. The caller’s question about treatment safety goes to your technician.

Read the full sample transcript
  1. Agent

    GreetingThanks for calling Oak Valley Pest Control, this is Dana. How can I help?

  2. Caller

    We’re hearing scratching in the kitchen wall, and there are droppings under the sink.

  3. Agent

    Pest & urgencyI can help with that. When did you first notice it, and is it just the kitchen?

  4. Caller

    About a week ago. Mostly the kitchen.

  5. Agent

    Service areaWhat’s the service address? I’ll make sure you’re in our area.

  6. Caller

    14 Oak Lane.✓ Inside your service area

  7. Agent

    Access & petsWill someone be home, and are there pets on the property?

  8. Caller

    I’ll be home. We have a dog in the back yard. Is the treatment safe for him?

  9. Agent

    Good question. Your technician will go over that with you on site, and I’ll note there’s a dog so they’re prepared.

  10. Agent

    BookI have tomorrow between 10am and 12pm. Does that work?

  11. Caller

    Yes, perfect.✓ Booked from your approved slots

  12. Agent

    ConfirmYou’re all set for tomorrow between 10 and 12. Is this the best number to reach you? … Great. Anything else I can help with?

Three common call situations

  1. A new customer asks for a visit.

    We note what they report, check the address against your service area and offer one of your approved slots.

    You getA booked visit with the details your technician needs, or a handoff when the request falls outside your rules.

  2. An existing customer needs a change.

    We reschedule or note the request by your rules. Cancellations and billing questions go to you unless you tell us otherwise.

    You getA short note of what they asked for and what we changed.

  3. A call needs your judgment.

    Urgent requests, callers outside your area, warranty or billing questions: we take the details and the reason, and make no promises on your behalf.

    You getThe call flagged to your named contact, the way you choose.
