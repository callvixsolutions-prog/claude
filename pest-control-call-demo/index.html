Questions owners ask before booking

What hours are covered?

The base plan covers Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm in your time zone. Evening coverage is an optional $199/month add-on and weekend coverage is $149/month, both during windows confirmed with you before you start. Overnight coverage is not available.

What happens when my office is already on a call?

You forward the calls you choose — all of them, or only the ones your team cannot pick up. Those calls reach us instead of ringing out, and are handled under the same rules.

Can you book appointments directly, and how does my CRM fit in?

Yes. If the address is in your service area, the request fits your rules and a slot is free, the appointment is booked from your availability. Booking and call details then reach your team using the method agreed during setup, plus a weekly activity summary.

With direct CRM entry ($99/month) our team enters agreed call and booking details in your own system, using access you grant and can revoke. This is our representatives working in your account — not a native integration, an automatic sync, or a partnership with your CRM vendor. We confirm access and the booking workflow for your system before setup.

Who answers my calls, and where are they based?

A trained person, every time. Callvix does not use an AI voice bot on client calls. Our team includes representatives based in Cairo, Egypt, selected for English communication and trained on pest control terminology and your own services, service area, pricing and booking rules. Our business address in Marlton, New Jersey is a business address, not a call center.

What happens with calls outside my rules?

They are routed to you rather than guessed at. By default our representatives do not take payment, quote account balances or process cancellations — those are recorded with the reason and escalated under your instructions. Where you have explicitly told us to handle something, we follow that instead.

How does setup work?

We agree your rules, you forward the numbers you choose, we run test calls together, then coverage starts on the window you agreed. Timing depends on your phone setup and approvals, so we agree it with you rather than publishing a number.

How is the price calculated, and is there a contract?

$399/month base plus $2.99 for each inbound call we answer, and $0.99 for each outbound follow-up when used. Optional services are evening $199/month, weekend $149/month and direct CRM entry $99/month. Your monthly total is the base, the calls handled and any options you choose. There is no long-term contract — it is month to month.
