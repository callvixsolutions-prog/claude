Setting it up

What we need from you before we answer in your name

Four steps. The first one is a conversation, not a form.

Agree the rules. Coverage window, service area, what may be booked, the pricing you allow us to quote, and who urgent calls go to.

Forward the numbers you choose. All calls, or only the ones your office does not pick up. Your existing number stays yours.

Run test calls together. We correct the wording and routing before any customer reaches us.

Coverage starts on the window you agreed, once the test calls look right.

Setup timing depends on your phone setup, your rules and your approvals, so we agree it with you rather than promising a number here.

Working in your CRM. With the CRM Management add-on ($99/month) our representatives enter call notes and booking details directly in your own account — FieldRoutes, ServiceTitan, GorillaDesk, Briostack, PestPac, Housecall Pro or whatever you run — using a login you grant and can revoke. This is a person working in your system, not a native integration or an automatic data sync, and there is no partnership with any CRM vendor. Without the add-on, no account access is needed.

FAQs

The questions owners actually ask

Who actually answers the phone?

A trained person, every time. Callvix does not use an AI voice bot on client calls. Representatives learn your services, service area, pricing and booking rules before they answer in your name.

What happens when my office is already on a call?

You forward the calls you choose — all of them, or only the ones your team does not pick up. Overflow reaches us instead of ringing out, and is handled under the same rules.

What hours are covered, and can you do evenings or weekends?

The base plan covers Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm in your local time. Evening coverage is an optional $199/month add-on during an agreed window, and weekend coverage is $149/month during agreed windows. There is no overnight coverage and no blanket 24/7 plan.

Can you book jobs directly, or do you just take a message?

We book. If the address is in your service area, the job fits the rules you set and a slot is free, the appointment is booked. If any of that does not hold, the call is escalated to you rather than guessed at.

How does this work with my CRM?

With the CRM Management add-on ($99/month) our representatives work inside your own account using a login you grant and can revoke, entering call notes and booking details after the call. It is manual work by a person — not a native integration, not an automatic sync, and not a partnership with your CRM vendor. Without the add-on, booking and call details reach your team using the method agreed during setup.

What happens with urgent calls, or calls outside my rules?

Urgency is recorded and the call follows your escalation route. By default our representatives do not take payment, quote account balances or process cancellations — those are captured and escalated under your instructions. Where you have explicitly told us to handle something, we follow that instead.

How is the price calculated?

$399/month base, plus $2.99 for each inbound call we answer and $0.99 for each outbound follow-up we place, charged only when used. Optional add-ons are evening $199/month, weekend $149/month and CRM Management $99/month. The per-call rate is fixed regardless of call length; your total still moves with call volume and the add-ons you choose.

How long does setup take?

It depends on your phone setup, your rules and your approvals, so we agree it with you rather than publishing a number. The sequence is: agree the rules, forward the numbers you choose, run test calls together, then coverage starts.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Month to month. You can add, reduce or cancel coverage as your business changes.

What actually happens on the 20-minute call?

We go through how your calls are handled now and where they are being missed, show you how we would answer them, agree what we could book without checking with you first, and give you a cost estimate based on your own call volume. It is a conversation, not a pitch, and there is nothing to sign.
