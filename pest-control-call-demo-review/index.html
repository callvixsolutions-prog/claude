BEFORE YOU HAND OVER THE PHONE

Questions before you book

A few things you may want to know before we talk.

Can you answer only when our office is busy?

Yes. You choose during setup whether to forward every call or only the calls your office does not answer. Either way the calls that reach us are handled under the same agreed instructions.

Can you book in our existing system?

Appointments are booked from the availability you approve, and the booking and call details then reach your team using the method agreed during setup. With direct CRM entry at $99/month, our representatives enter those details in your own account using access you grant and can revoke. That is a person working in your system — not a native integration, an automatic sync, or a partnership with your software provider.

What hours can you cover?

The base plan covers Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm in your time zone. Evening coverage is $199/month and weekend coverage is $149/month, during windows agreed before service starts. There is no overnight coverage.

Who answers the calls?

Real people, trained on pest-control terminology and on your own services, service area and booking rules. Callvix does not use an AI voice bot on client calls.

What happens when a caller needs our approval?

The request is recorded with the reason and escalated to the contact you nominated. By default our representatives do not take payment, quote account balances or process cancellations. Where you have told us explicitly to handle something, we follow that instead.

Is there a long-term contract, and how does setup work?

Month to month, with no long-term contract. Setup is: agree your instructions, forward the calls you choose, run test calls together, then coverage starts. Timing depends on your phone setup and approvals, so it is confirmed with you rather than published as a fixed number.
