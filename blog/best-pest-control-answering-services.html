Callvix Solutions

Callvix Solutions is a human answering service built only for U.S. pest control companies. Trained people answer in your company name, check the caller’s address against your service area, qualify the pest issue and book eligible jobs by your rules. Urgent calls are escalated to the person you name.

Base coverage is Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm in your local time. After-Hours Coverage ($199/month) and Weekend Coverage ($149/month) are add-ons; overnight answering is not offered. Pricing is $399/month plus $2.99 per answered inbound call and $0.99 per outbound follow-up, billed only when someone answers or we leave a voicemail. There’s no setup fee and no long-term contract: see the full pricing.

With the CRM Management add-on ($99/month), our representative enters bookings in your own FieldRoutes, PestPac, GorillaDesk, Briostack, ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro account, using a named user you create. It’s entry by a person; there is no native integration or automatic sync. See how calls are handled, step by step.

Ruby

Ruby offers 24/7 live answering, including after hours, weekends and holidays, and publishes a pest control page. Plans run from $250 a month for 50 minutes to $1,725 for 500 minutes, billed in 60-second increments; the rate for extra minutes isn’t published. Ruby says there’s no long-term contract and no activation, onboarding or setup fees.

Specialty Answering Service

Specialty Answering Service offers 24/7/365 live answering billed by the second. The Economy plan is $44 a month plus $1.54 a minute, and minute bundles start at $159 for 100 minutes, with overage of $1.09 to $1.44 a minute depending on the plan. Service is month-to-month with $0 setup and a 14-day free trial.

MAP Communications

MAP Communications offers 24/7 live answering and publishes a pest control page. Its Business plan is $179 a month for 125 minutes, billed in whole minutes, with $1.30 per extra minute. Its FAQ describes month-to-month service with 30 days’ notice to cancel, and its terms list a per-call telecom fee and a holiday fee.

AnswerForce

AnswerForce offers 24/7 live answering by people and says callers never get AI or voicemail. It bills by the minute, and plan prices are shown only after you register; its support pages describe a month-by-month rolling contract. It names field-service software such as ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro and Jobber, and no pest-specific system.

PATLive

PATLive offers 24/7/365 live answering billed by the minute, then in 6-second increments. Plans start at $49 a month plus $2.99 a minute (pay as you go) or $99 for 50 minutes, and overage falls from $2.29 to $1.49 a minute on larger plans. It uses no contracts and offers a 14-day free trial.

Smith.ai (human tier)

Smith.ai’s human receptionist plans are priced per call: $300 a month for 30 calls ($11.50 per extra call), $810 for 90 calls ($10.50) or $2,100 for 300 calls ($8.50). Service is month-to-month with no setup fees and staffed 24/7. Its pest control page names PestPac, ServSuite and FieldRoutes, and it has a separate GorillaDesk page.

Sameday AI is an AI answering service for pest control companies that answers 24/7 and says it routes anything truly emergent to your on-call technician. My AI Front Desk

My AI Front Desk is an AI receptionist that answers 24/7. Its Business-in-a-Box plan is $99 a month with 200 voice minutes, then $0.25 a minute in credits, with no contracts and a 7-day free trial. Its pest control pages name FieldRoutes, PestPac, GorillaDesk and Briostack.

AgentZap

AgentZap is an AI receptionist with an on-demand live-agent network. Smith.ai (AI tier)

Abby Connect lets you choose human receptionists, AI or both, billed in Abby Minutes; a minute of AI answering uses half an Abby Minute. SignpostAI (formerly Signpost) offers AI and live receptionists around the clock; its pricing page states a 6-month agreement with no setup fees, and it names Jobber, Housecall Pro and ServiceTitan.

When Callvix may not fit

Callvix doesn’t offer overnight answering; if you need live answering between late evening and early morning, look for a 24/7 provider.

How to choose

  • Pest-only training. Ask whether the people (or the AI) answering have been trained on pest control calls, not general reception.
  • Booking by your rules. Ask how the service checks your service area and which slots it may offer, and what it does with a call that doesn’t fit.
  • Your CRM. Ask how bookings reach your system: by a person in your account, by a connection the vendor describes, or by text and email.
  • Per call or per minute, at your call length. Price a month with your own call count and average call length; our guide to answering service pricing explains billing units.
  • Contract terms. Check the notice period, setup fees and what happens to a part month when you cancel.

Frequently asked questions

Is a human or an AI answering service better for pest control?

It depends on your calls. AI can answer at any hour and handle simple intake at a low price per minute; trained people can use judgment on urgent pest issues, service-area questions and booking exceptions. Callvix Solutions is human-answered: trained people answer in your company name and book by your rules. Test any service with your own calls before you decide.

Is per-minute or per-call pricing cheaper?

It depends on how long your calls run. Per-minute plans cost more as calls get longer; per-call pricing stays the same whatever the length. Callvix Solutions is $399/month plus $2.99 per answered inbound call, and $0.99 per outbound follow-up when used. Month-to-month, no setup fee. Compare that with per-minute plans using your own average call length.

Do I need a 24/7 answering service?

Only if your call logs show bookable calls arriving overnight that you are losing. Callvix Solutions covers Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm in your local time, with evening and weekend coverage as add-ons. It doesn’t offer overnight answering, so if you need live answering overnight, look for a 24/7 provider.

What should I ask an answering service before signing?

Ask how a billable call or minute is defined, the overage rate, any setup or onboarding fee, the contract length and notice period, holiday rates, and exactly what the agent may book or promise on your behalf. Callvix Solutions publishes all of its prices, and our 25-question answering service checklist covers the rest.

Will an answering service work with my pest control CRM?

It depends on the service. Some AI services describe direct connections to software such as FieldRoutes or PestPac. Callvix Solutions sends booking details to your office by text or email, and with the CRM Management add-on ($99/month) a trained person enters them in your own FieldRoutes, PestPac, GorillaDesk, Briostack, ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro account. It’s manual entry; there is no native integration or automatic sync.
