Pest Control Call Volume by Season and Region

By Callvix Solutions6 min readPublished 24 October 2026

Pest activity is seasonal, and the calls that reach your office follow it. Termites swarm in spring. Stinging insects get more defensive in late summer. Some pests move indoors in fall. The timing shifts by region, so your busy months depend on where you work.

What this guide is, and isn’t: it shows when the pests behind common calls are active in each region, using university extension services and NPMA. It does not report call-volume figures. Your own call logs are the only source for your own numbers.

Two patterns that apply nationwide

  • Spring is termite season. NPMA calls spring “prime termite season” (NPMA). The exact months vary by region and species, as the table shows.
  • Rodents move indoors as it gets colder. NPMA notes that as temperatures drop, rodents across the country push their way indoors in search of shelter, food and water (NPMA).

What your phones need to handle in a busy season

The pests change by region. The call-handling questions don’t change much.

  • Ask the urgency questions first. For stinging insects and scorpions, ask whether anyone has been stung before anything else, and route a possible emergency the way your safety script says. Our after-hours call-handling plan sets out four escalation levels, including when to direct a caller to 911 or Poison Help.
  • Separate inspection requests from treatment requests. A caller who has seen swarmers usually needs an inspection booked, not a price. Record what the caller reports and where, and let your technician confirm the pest. Our call script and intake form lists the fields to capture.
  • Plan for evenings and weekends. Formosan termites swarm around dusk, and weekend calls don’t wait for Monday. Decide which windows are answered live and which are captured for a callback. Callvix base coverage is Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm in your company’s local time; evening ($199/month) and weekend ($149/month) coverage are optional add-ons.
  • Know what an unanswered call is worth in your busy months. Our guide to what missed calls cost a pest control business uses four numbers from your own records.
  • Get bookings into your system. In a busy month, notes that sit outside your CRM get lost. With the optional CRM Management add-on ($99/month), a Callvix representative enters call notes and booking details in your own FieldRoutes, PestPac, GorillaDesk, Briostack, ServiceTitan and Housecall Pro account, using access you grant. It’s manual entry by a person, not an automatic sync.

Questions

When is termite season?

Spring, broadly: NPMA calls spring prime termite season. Timing varies by region and species. Penn State says swarms in Pennsylvania usually emerge between February and June. LSU AgCenter puts Formosan termite swarms in Louisiana from April through June, peaking in early to mid May. In California, UC IPM says subterranean termites swarm after fall or spring rains.

When do stinging-insect calls rise?

Late summer into fall. Penn State notes that bees and wasps become more defensive later in the summer and into fall, and UC IPM says yellowjacket defensive behavior increases as the season progresses.

Does this guide give call-volume numbers?

No. It uses published pest-activity timing from university extension services and NPMA, not call-volume data. To see your own busy months, use your call logs. Our guide to what missed calls cost shows a simple four-input method.
