Free tools

Free tools for pest control offices

Three free tools for pest control offices. Download or print them, adapt them to your rules and use them with any provider. No email or sign-up required.

They exist because the decisions they cover — who answers your phone, what that person says, and what happens at 9pm — are usually made without a written standard to compare against.

The tools

  • Answering service vendor scorecard PDF, 5 pagesA printable scorecard carrying all 25 questions to ask a provider, with space for evidence, a 0–10 score and a decision summary. Built to be used across several vendors, including ones that are not Callvix.
  • Call script and customer intake form Print or save as PDFA template for what your office says when the phone rings and what it writes down: the pest issue, the address, urgency, and the details a technician needs before the visit.
  • After-hours escalation checklist Printable checklistThe levels of urgency an after-hours call can fall into, what each one should trigger, and who gets woken up. Useful whether the calls are handled in-house or by a provider.

Nothing here asks for an email address. If a tool is useful, link to it.
