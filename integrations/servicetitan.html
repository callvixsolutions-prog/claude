How it works

What happens to a call, step by step

The base service covers answering in your company’s name, qualification and booking by your rules, Monday–Friday 9am–6pm your company’s local time, with evening and weekend coverage as options. Booking and call details reach your team by the method agreed during setup. The steps below are the work inside ServiceTitan that the optional CRM Management add-on ($99/month) adds.

1

Lookup and context

Customer and location search before anything else, with membership status read before we discuss pricing or availability.

2

New customer and location

Created with the campaign or lead-source information available, recorded in the fields and categories your office approves.

3

The job

We use the business unit, job type and arrival window allowed by your booking rules.

4

Job notes

Pest, severity, structure and access details written where your dispatcher and tech both read them.

5

Escalation

Commercial work, membership changes, anything with a price outside your published range, and any call your rules flag goes straight to your named contact.

System specifics

Where ServiceTitan changes how we work

Business unit and job type mapping is agreed in writing at onboarding. If a call doesn't map cleanly we escalate rather than guess, because an incorrect job type can create a booking or reporting error.

We record the available campaign or lead-source information in the fields and categories your office approves. Unknown or conflicting sources are flagged rather than guessed.

Membership and plan status is checked, using the information available to us, before pricing is discussed, and pricing follows your approved rules. Unclear cases go to your named contact.

Arrival windows are booked to your capacity rules, not to the first opening.

In practice

Three example ServiceTitan calls, and how we’d handle them

Illustrative scenarios — your own booking and escalation rules decide the actual response.

The paid-search caller

If you're spending on lead generation, the source of this call matters. We capture the source details available for the call using your agreed mapping. Your office can combine those records with campaign spend and completed-job outcomes when reviewing marketing performance.

“I'm a member — does that cover this?”

Membership status is checked, using the information available to us, before any pricing is discussed, and members are quoted by your approved pricing rules. Unclear cases go to your named contact, as in the example call above. Membership changes and cancellation requests are recorded and escalated under your instructions.

“We manage twelve properties”

Property management and commercial calls are qualified properly — sites, contacts, scope, current provider — and escalated with a written summary rather than being forced into a residential job type.
