What we do

Your phone, handled like an in-house office

Callvix is a pest control answering service staffed by trained people, not an AI bot. During your agreed coverage we answer in your company’s name, qualify the pest issue, and book eligible jobs by your rules. With the CRM Management add-on ($99/month) we also work inside GorillaDesk itself, so your team sees the job where they expect it. Standard coverage is Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm in your local time; evening and weekend coverage are optional add-ons.

System specifics

Where GorillaDesk changes how we work

A small team may prefer not to expose the whole calendar. A defined daily booking window, with everything else escalated, is a sensible way to start — and easy to loosen later.

Job durations matter when one tech is the whole schedule. We book the length you tell us for each job type, not a default.

If you use tags for lead source or campaign tracking, we apply them from the source information available, using your approved value when the source is unknown.

How call details reach you is agreed during setup. That's a standard option, not an upgrade.

In practice

Three example GorillaDesk calls, and how we’d handle them

Illustrative scenarios — your own booking and escalation rules decide the actual response.

“Can someone come today?”

The scenario in the example call above. With one or two techs, same-day may not be possible, so we offer the earliest slot you've opened, set the expectation clearly, and flag urgency so you can move it if the route allows.

“What do you charge?”

An owner-operator can lose a caller here when the price conversation has to wait for the owner. We quote from your set range, and anything outside it comes to you with the details already captured.

The caller who's already been quoted by two competitors

Price-shopping calls need different handling than urgent ones. We pass your team the caller's requirements and any comparison details they choose to share.
