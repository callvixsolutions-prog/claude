System specifics

Where Briostack changes how we work

Cancellation and plan-change calls are the ones you least want a call center improvising on. Our default is to capture, reassure and escalate rather than process a cancellation.

During setup, we identify which permitted actions may trigger customer messages and agree which actions representatives should avoid or escalate.

Re-service and warranty calls may have different eligibility and billing rules; we follow the rule you approve or escalate unclear cases.

We record the lead-source information available under your agreed mapping, using your approved value when the source is unknown.

In practice

Three example Briostack calls, and how we’d handle them

Illustrative scenarios — your own booking and escalation rules decide the actual response.

“I want to cancel my plan”

A request that needs clear ownership and a careful handoff, as in the example call above. We listen, capture the reason in the customer's own words, explain how the office will follow up under your instructions, and escalate. By default we don’t process the cancellation.

“Why was I charged this month?”

Billing questions on recurring accounts are recorded and escalated under your instructions, with the caller's question in their own words. We don't quote balances.

“Nobody showed up”

A missed appointment call is a retention event. We confirm what the record shows, apologize on your behalf without admitting fault or promising a credit, and escalate with the full timeline, under your escalation instructions.
