What we do

System specifics

Where Briostack changes how we work

Cancellation and plan-change calls are the ones you least want a call center improvising on. Our default is to capture, reassure and escalate rather than process a cancellation.

Where your office runs automated reminders and follow-ups, we avoid actions that would double up on a sequence the customer is already in.

Re-service and warranty calls are booked or escalated exactly as you specify, since those affect margin rather than revenue.

Every new customer we create carries the lead source your reporting depends on.

In practice

Three example Briostack calls, and how we’d handle them

Illustrative scenarios — your own booking and escalation rules decide the actual response.

“I want to cancel my plan”

The highest-stakes call your phone gets, and the one you least want improvised. We listen, capture the real reason in the customer's own words, tell them someone will call back, and escalate under your escalation instructions. By default we don’t process the cancellation.

“Why was I charged this month?”

Billing questions on recurring accounts get captured and escalated, never answered. We don't quote balances, and a wrong answer here costs more than the call.

“Nobody showed up”

A missed appointment call is a retention event. We confirm what the record shows, apologise on your behalf without admitting fault or promising a credit, and escalate with the full timeline, under your escalation instructions.
