What we do

Your phone, handled like an in-house office

Callvix is a pest control answering service staffed by trained people, not an AI bot. During your agreed coverage we answer in your company’s name, qualify the pest issue, and book eligible jobs by your rules. With the CRM Management add-on ($99/month) we also work inside Housecall Pro itself, so your team sees the job where they expect it. Standard coverage is Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm in your local time; evening and weekend coverage are optional add-ons.

System specifics

Where Housecall Pro changes how we work

Housecall Pro isn't built around pest-specific service types, so the mapping work matters more, not less. We agree job types and durations for each pest scenario in writing before we take a live call.

If you use the estimate flow, we capture the details and hand it over rather than pricing work ourselves.

Tags are applied consistently so your follow-up and marketing filters keep working.

Recurring service is offered only where you've told us to offer it, with your wording.

In practice

Three calls a Housecall Pro office gets every week

The ones where the difference between a trained pest-control representative and a generic call center shows up in your revenue rather than in a survey.

“Can you just give me a ballpark?”

Housecall Pro offices often run an estimate flow rather than fixed pricing. We give a range only where you've authorised one, capture everything an estimate needs, and hand it over rather than pricing the job ourselves.

“It's a rental — the tenant will let you in”

Access and authorisation calls create no-shows. We capture who is paying, who is authorising, who will be on site, and how the tech gets in — and put all four in the job notes.

“Do you do wildlife too?”

Scope questions where the honest answer may be no. We answer from your actual service list rather than saying yes to keep the caller happy, and escalate anything adjacent that you might want.
