Plan terms

  • No setup fee. Month-to-month, with no long-term contract. Cancel any time by email; service continues to the end of that billing month, and part months aren’t refunded.
  • Billed per call, not per minute. Answered inbound calls are billed, including wrong numbers and repeat calls. Spam and robocalls, missed calls and hang-ups before we answer aren’t billed.
  • Your configuration and billing details are confirmed in writing before coverage begins. Each invoice comes with an itemized record of billed calls.
