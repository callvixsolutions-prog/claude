Billing questions

Questions about billing

What counts as a billable call?

Answered inbound calls, whatever their length, including wrong numbers and repeat calls. Spam and robocalls aren’t billed, and neither are missed calls or callers who hang up before we answer. Each invoice comes with an itemized record of billed calls.

Are outbound follow-up calls billed?

Yes, but only when someone answers or we leave a voicemail.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Callvix is month-to-month. You can cancel any time by email; service continues to the end of that billing month, and part months aren’t refunded.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Your configuration and billing details are confirmed in writing before coverage begins, and you’re typically live 7–10 business days after we receive your details.
