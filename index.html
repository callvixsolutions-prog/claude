The cost of a ringing phone

In pest control, a missed call can mean a missed booking

Pest problems feel urgent. A wasp nest by the front door, mice in the walls, ants across the counter — the caller wants it dealt with today, and when nobody answers they can simply ring the next company.

Nothing about that is a failure of effort. It is what happens when the people who do the work are also the people expected to answer.

How it works

From first call to booked job

Every call runs in the same order, and every decision in it follows a rule you set. See how setup and test calls work →

Your rules stay in charge

Agreed with you before we answer a single call.

  1. Your service areaZIPs or a radius, checked before anything is booked.
  2. What we may quoteThe services and prices you allow us to discuss. Everything else goes to you.
  3. Which slots we may offerHow far ahead, which windows, and what we never book.

When a call isn’t a booking

Urgent
→ Your named contact, by the method you choose
Outside your area
→ Logged and returned to you, never booked
Warranty or a judgment call
→ Escalated. We don’t promise on your behalf

Questions

Straight answers for pest control owners

Is Callvix a real human service or an AI bot?

Real people. Trained representatives answer in your company’s name. Callvix is not an AI service and does not use a voice bot.

Do you answer calls as our company?

Yes. We answer in your business name, using the greeting agreed during setup.

What hours are covered?

The $399 base covers Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm in your local time. Evenings and weekends are separately priced add-ons.

Which CRM or scheduling systems do you work with?

Our representatives work inside the system your office already runs, using a login you grant and can revoke. That is human work in your account — there is no native integration and no automatic data sync.
