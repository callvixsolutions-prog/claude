The cost of a ringing phone

In pest control, a missed call is often a lost customer.

Pest problems feel urgent — a wasp nest, mice in the walls, ants across the counter. When the first company doesn't answer, many callers simply contact the next available provider instead of waiting or leaving a voicemail.

The problem can't wait

A wasp nest or mice in the walls feels like an emergency. If you don't pick up, the caller just tries the next company.

Voicemail won't save it

Most people won't leave a message about an urgent problem. They hang up and dial the next number on the list.

First to answer wins

Picking up live, on the first ring, is often what books the job — before a competitor ever gets the chance.

One call keeps paying

A single new customer can turn into recurring service — so every call you recover keeps adding up over time.
