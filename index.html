The cost of a ringing phone

In pest control, a missed call is often a lost customer

Pest problems feel urgent. A wasp nest by the front door, mice in the walls, ants across the counter — the caller wants it dealt with today, not after a callback. When the first company doesn’t answer, many callers simply ring the next one rather than wait or leave a voicemail.

You know the pattern. You are mid-treatment, driving between stops, or the office is already stretched, and the phone just rings. Nothing about that is a failure of effort; it is what happens when the people who do the work are also the people expected to answer.

How it works

From first call to booked job

Callvix works only with pest control companies, so representatives learn the language callers use — ants vs. carpenter ants, one-time vs. quarterly, WDO inspections, “is it safe for my dog?” — and your rules before they answer in your name.

Agreed before we answer a single call

Your services and what we may quoteThe prices you allow us to give, and the work we hand to you instead.
Your service areaZIPs or a radius, and the question that checks it before anything is booked.
Your booking rulesWhich slots we may offer, how far ahead, and what we never book.

And where a call goes when it is not a booking

Urgent→Your named contact, by the method you choose
Outside your area→Logged and returned to you, never booked
Warranty or a judgment call→Escalated. We do not promise on your behalf

Questions

Straight answers for pest control owners

Is Callvix a real human service or an AI bot?

Real people. Trained representatives answer in your company’s name. Callvix is not an AI service and does not use a voice bot.

Do you answer calls as our company?

Yes. We answer in your business name, using the greeting agreed during setup.

What hours are covered?

The $399 base covers Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm in your local time. Evenings and weekends are separately priced add-ons.

Which CRM or scheduling systems do you work with?

Our representatives work inside the system your office already runs, using a login you grant and can revoke. That is human work in your account — there is no native integration and no automatic data sync.
