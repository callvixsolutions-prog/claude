The cost of a ringing phone

In pest control, a missed call is often a lost customer

Pest problems feel urgent. A wasp nest by the front door, mice in the walls, ants across the counter — the caller wants it dealt with today, not after a callback. When the first company doesn’t answer, many callers simply ring the next one rather than wait or leave a voicemail.

You know the pattern. You are mid-treatment, driving between stops, or the office is already stretched, and the phone just rings. Nothing about that is a failure of effort; it is what happens when the people who do the work are also the people expected to answer.

Callvix covers that gap during your agreed coverage hours. A trained person answers in your company’s name, qualifies the pest issue, and books the job or escalates it by the rules you set — so the caller gets a person rather than a voicemail, and you get the job rather than your competitor.

If you want to put a number on it, our guide to what missed calls really cost walks through the arithmetic using your own booking rate and job value rather than an industry average.

Pricing

Callvix costs $399 a month plus a flat $2.99 per call — not per minute

Callvix charges U.S. pest control companies a $399 monthly base, a flat $2.99 for each inbound call answered and $0.99 for each outbound follow-up placed, as of October 2026. The per-call rate is fixed rather than metered by the minute, so a long, difficult call costs the same as a quick one. Your total rises with call volume and with any add-ons you choose.
