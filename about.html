Why Callvix Solutions

Human call answering, built around pest control

Callvix Solutions exists to help pest control companies capture opportunities that come through the phone. Every account is set up hands-on around your services, service areas, pricing and booking rules.

What makes us different

Four things that shape how we work

  • Trained people, not a bot

    Pest calls are often urgent and need judgment, so a person answers.

  • Pest control only

    Representatives learn pest control terminology, urgency and booking before they take a live call.

  • Answered in your name

    Callers hear your company name and greeting, not a third-party answering-service line.

  • Per-call pricing, not per-minute billing

    $399/month base, $2.99 per answered inbound call and $0.99 per outbound follow-up when used. No long-term contract. See pricing and optional add-ons

How we deliver quality

How we learn your account and run the calls

A missed call can mean a missed booking, so our answering service for pest control companies works as an extension of your office: answering in your company name and following the workflow and script agreed with you while your team stays on the truck.

Agents are selected for clear English, attentive listening and a professional phone manner, then trained on pest control and on your account before they answer in your name.

See the setup steps

  1. Learn your account

    Your greeting, services, service areas, pricing, booking rules and escalation instructions are documented and agreed with you before representatives answer in your name.

  2. Test before launch

    We run test calls with you and correct the greeting, wording and routing before coverage starts.

  3. Record the outcome

In detail

How your account is set up and run

The same steps apply to every account. Nothing goes live until you have agreed the rules and heard the test calls.

How a new account is set up

Setup starts with a discovery call about your services, hours, availability and call-handling rules. Then we write the rules down with you: your coverage window, your service areas and what to do with an address outside them, the pricing you allow us to quote, what may be booked and what must come to you, and who receives escalations and how.

You forward the numbers you choose. That can be all calls, or only the ones your office doesn’t pick up, so your existing number stays yours. Your configuration and billing details are confirmed in writing before coverage begins. Typically, you’re live 7–10 business days after we receive your details.

How representatives are trained for your account

Representatives complete pest control training first: the terminology, the urgency levels and the booking workflow. Then they learn your account: your services, service area, pricing and booking rules, and the greeting callers will hear.

Before any customer reaches us, we run test calls with you. We correct the greeting, the wording and the routing until the calls sound the way you want them to.

Call recording and reporting

Calls are recorded, with reporting on the calls we handle. Booking and call details reach your team by text or email, or by the method agreed during setup. An appointment is described to a caller as confirmed only once it has actually been reserved. Each invoice comes with an itemized record of billed calls.

Escalation to you or your office

Anything outside your rules is captured and handed back to you rather than guessed at. A warranty question, a price outside your list or an address outside your area is escalated. Urgent calls reach the person you name, by the route you choose. Billing questions, payment requests and cancellation requests are recorded and escalated under your instructions. We don’t promise a warranty visit on your behalf.

Base hours and add-ons

Base coverage is Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm in your company’s local time. Evening coverage ($199/month) and weekend coverage ($149/month) are optional add-ons, with the windows agreed before service starts. Overnight coverage is not offered.

With the optional CRM Management add-on ($99/month), a representative enters agreed call notes and booking details in your own system, using access you grant and can revoke. There is no native integration or automatic sync. The service itself is $399/month plus $2.99 per answered inbound call and $0.99 per outbound follow-up when used, with no long-term contract.
