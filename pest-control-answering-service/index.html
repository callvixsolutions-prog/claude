FAQ

Questions owners ask before starting.

Are the people answering real?

Yes. Your calls are answered by real people, not an automated system. They answer in your company’s name using the information and booking rules you approve.

Can you answer only when our team is busy?

Yes. You can forward all calls, or only the ones your office doesn’t pick up. The setup depends on your phone system and the coverage you choose.

What if I need evening or weekend coverage?

The base service covers Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm in your time zone. Evening and weekend coverage are optional add-ons (+$199/month and +$149/month), and we agree the exact hours with you before you start.

Can we keep our existing phone number?

Yes. You forward your existing number to us, and you can stop forwarding at any time.

How do you follow our services, prices and booking rules?

During setup we learn your services, the prices you allow us to quote, your service area, booking rules and escalation contacts. You approve the call-handling instructions before anything goes live, and calls are handled from them.

Can you work with our scheduling or CRM system?

We work inside the system your office already uses, with access you grant and can revoke. Direct CRM entry is an optional $99/month add-on: a person enters customer details, notes and bookings in your agreed system. It isn’t a native integration or automatic sync, and we confirm the workflow with you first.

How are urgent or unusual calls handled?

We follow your escalation instructions and contact the person you name. Pest identification, treatment recommendations and safety advice stay with your team.

What do we need to provide before starting?

Your greeting and the questions callers should be asked, your services and the prices we may quote, your service area and booking windows, and who to contact for urgent calls. CRM access only if you choose direct CRM entry.
