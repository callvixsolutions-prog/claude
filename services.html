Getting started

What setup involves

Four steps, starting with a conversation about how your office already runs.

  1. Agree the rules

    Coverage window, service area, what may be booked, pricing you allow us to quote, and who urgent calls go to. Our call script and intake form shows the questions involved.

  2. Set up forwarding and access

    You forward the numbers you choose, and we agree the method for sharing booking and call details. If you take CRM Management, you also create a login in your own system with the permissions you’re comfortable granting.

  3. Test calls

    We run calls through the rules together and correct the wording and routing before any customer reaches us.

  4. Coverage starts

    Live answering begins on the window you agreed, once the test calls look right.

The same rules, three different calls

Not every call is a new job. What happens next depends on the rules you set, not on the handler’s guess.

  • Booked

    A new customer, inside your area

    “Wasps by the back door.” We capture the pest, the urgency and the address, check it against your service area, and offer a slot you have opened. Flagged urgent, with access and pet notes for the technician.

  • Escalated

    An existing customer calling back

    “You treated last week and they’re back.” We record who is calling, the address and what they are seeing. Whether a return visit is covered is your decision, so it goes to you by the route you chose. We don’t promise a warranty visit on your behalf.

  • Returned

    A caller outside your area

    “Do you come out to us?” We take the address and check it before anything else. If it falls outside the ZIPs or radius you gave us, no slot is offered: it is logged and returned to you, or handled by the rule you set for out-of-area calls.

Illustrative examples, not recordings, records of real calls or customer testimonials. A call only ends in a booking when the address meets your service-area rules and a slot is actually free; otherwise it is escalated or returned to you by the rule you set.

Human-led

A trained person answers, not an automated menu

No “press 1 for scheduling.” Representatives complete pest control and account-specific training, and learn your services, service area, pricing and booking rules before they answer in your name. We run test calls with you and correct the wording and routing before any customer reaches us.

We set out where an automated system fits and where it does not in our comparison of AI and human answering, written to be useful whichever you choose.

  1. Your greeting

    Calls open in your business name, with the greeting agreed during setup.

  2. Location and service checks

    The address is checked against your service area, and the request against the services you offer, before anything is promised.

  3. Approved scheduling rules

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a pest control answering service?

It’s a phone-answering team that handles your pest control calls in your company’s name — qualifying the pest issue, checking your service area, and booking the job by your rules. Callvix does this with trained people, and shares booking and call details with your team using the method agreed during setup. Direct entry into your CRM is the CRM Management add-on.

Is it 24/7?

No. Standard coverage is Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm in your local time. Optional evening and weekend coverage is available during the windows agreed at setup. Overnight coverage is not offered.

Is it human or AI?

Human. Calls are answered by trained people, not an AI voice bot, because pest calls are often urgent and emotional, and judgment matters.

How much does it cost?

$399/month base (Mon–Fri live answering and booking) plus a flat $2.99 per inbound call and $0.99 per outbound follow-up. Optional add-ons: After-Hours $199/mo, Weekend $149/mo, CRM Management $99/mo. No long-term contract. See the full pricing page.

What is an outbound follow-up?

An outbound call is a follow-up we place for you, as opposed to a customer call we answer. It costs $0.99 per call and is charged only when one is placed. Which follow-ups you want us to make is confirmed with your final configuration on your discovery call.

How do calls reach Callvix?

You forward the numbers you choose — all calls, or only the ones your office doesn’t pick up — so your existing number stays yours. We agree the rules first, run test calls, then coverage starts.

Can you work alongside my own office staff?

Yes. You can forward only overflow — the calls stacking up while the team is on a job — and keep answering the rest yourselves. You decide which calls come to us.

What happens to a call we can’t book?

It is captured and handed back to you rather than guessed at. Questions on pricing or scope outside your rules are escalated, cancellations are passed to your team with the reason, and callers outside your service area are handled by the rule you set.

Do you enter bookings into my CRM?

Booking the job by your rules is included in the base plan. Entering that booking and the call notes directly into your CRM is the CRM Management add-on, $99/month. With it our representatives work inside your own account — FieldRoutes, ServiceTitan, GorillaDesk, Briostack, PestPac or Housecall Pro — using access you grant and can revoke. If you use another system, tell us which one and we’ll confirm the workflow and access requirements during setup. There is no native integration or data sync.

What happens after hours?

Outside your coverage window, calls are handled by the arrangement agreed during setup. Add the After-Hours or Weekend coverage add-on and we answer and book live during those windows.
